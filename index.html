Smart Travel Advice

How to Save on International Trips

Essential insider tips to stretch your travel budget further without sacrificing comfort.

🗓️

Travel in Shoulder Seasons

Visit Europe in April–May or Sept–Oct instead of July. Flights and hotels are up to 40% cheaper, and popular landmarks have half the crowds.

🚆

Use Regional Rail Passes

In countries like Italy (Trenitalia) and Japan (JR Pass / regional IC cards), high-speed rail passes save hundreds over individual single tickets.

💳

Zero Forex Markup Cards

Never exchange currency at airports. Use a travel card with 0% foreign exchange fees to save 3.5% to 5% on every transaction.
