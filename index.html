Smart Travel Advice

How to Save on Multi-Country Trips

Insider advice for stitching together multi-city itineraries without blowing your budget.

🗺️

Use Open-Jaw "Multi-City" Flights

Instead of booking round-trip to Rome and back, book an "open-jaw" flight flying into Rome and flying out of Zurich. This saves hundreds in backtracking transport!

🚆

Cross Borders by High-Speed Rail

In Europe (Eurostar / TGV / Frecciarossa) and Japan (Shinkansen), high-speed rail is faster than flying airport-to-airport and drops you right in city centers.

💳

Combine High & Low-Cost Destinations

Pair an expensive country (like Switzerland) with a more budget-friendly neighbor (like Italy or Spain) to balance your overall average daily trip budget.
